Social Distance

I made the original clay sculpture during the 2020 Covid-19 pandemic. The 6-foot distance was the norm, and what better ways to express my frustration into something positive like a fluid foot with lots of negative space. Plus I always wanted to sculpt a foot.

Numbered and signed Roger Schinkler, limited edition of 11, with a Certificates of Authenticity.

Social Distance #11/11

Roger Schindler has an incredible ability to turn a simple concept into something both thought-provoking and memorable. His Social Distance foot sculpture is a perfect example-creative, clever, and reflective of a unique moment in our history. Roger’s work doesn’t just catch your eye; it starts a conversation. A truly talented artist with a distinctive vision. I will hold this piece with the most respect in my art collection as a reminder to stay positive when so much of the world was unknown.

– Drew Wilson

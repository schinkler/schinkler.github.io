Work in Progress

I was born in Stockholm, Sweden, where I went to school and was always interested in art. I later moved to the US and ended up going to school at the School of Visual Arts (SVA) in New York City. I now live in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, plus spending more time in Stockholm. Doing less and less graphic design, do have a part time job where I have more freedom to spend doing my fine art.

During the Covid-19 pandemic, I began rediscovering my passion for sculpture. I started recreating some of my older works in hydrocal, painting them with acrylics, and also exploring new sculptural forms in clay. You might wonder why I started this artistic journey later in life. The simple answer is a renewed sense of passion and the profound feeling of timelessness that accompanies the creative process.
